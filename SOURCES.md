# Public observation provenance

The first-refresh prior in `main.ts` uses two sources, both public:

1. The official kit's `competition.yaml`, comments on lines 100–114: unchanged early page with 638 tracked players and 943 awards, followed by a first refresh with 3,868 players and 4,276 awards. Both practice targets see that refreshed state. Subtracting the introduction trophy's 3,868 earners leaves 408 awards across the remaining trophies, versus 305 initially: 103 additional awards.
2. [Exophase's GTA VI PS5 page](https://www.exophase.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi-psn/trophies/), inspected on 19 November 2026: 14,028 players, 14,882 awards, 55 trophies, 4 platinum completions. The observed rates are recorded in `launchObservation` in Exophase list order. These are current public observations, not the first refresh's trophy-by-trophy rates or the final answer.

An Exophase rate rounded to two decimals implies a range of integer counts, not always a unique count. The prior carries low, middle, and high positions within those ranges, mixes initial-profile synchronization fractions and delay shapes, and conserves the first refresh's documented award total in each scenario before count discretization. The final revision models additional earners with a bounded binomial distribution. It preserves the allocated mean when it lies within the later observation's upper bound; marginal quantiles need not sum to the aggregate exactly. On the public practice inputs the final revision's displayed medians imply 4,269.5 awards, within rounding and marginalization tolerance of 4,276.

The exact refresh time of the current page was not available. Fifteen hours after release is an approximate observation age; the delay-power ensemble allows variation in how early awards accumulate. That timing assumption is a source of uncertainty. Public later observations can constrain a historical prior without identifying historical trophy rates uniquely.

The unsubmitted offline candidate additionally conditions the later integer-count intervals on the exact 14,882-award sum and conditions early acquisition distributions on the exact 103-additional-award sum. Its marginal median forecasts imply 4,270.3 awards; marginal medians do not have to sum to the joint total. The source page was checked again without a change in the observed data. A Wayback availability query for 2026-11-19 04:30 UTC returned no archived snapshot. No per-trophy practice answer was obtained.

The observation prior applies only to the documented, still-unseen launch refresh, with matching trophy metadata and a target no more than six hours ahead. The final week's acquisition model fits the history supplied by the grader; this later public snapshot is not injected into final inputs. The sealed reference dataset and rival source code were not accessed.

## Follow-up references

- [Exophase Xbox Series achievements](https://www.exophase.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi-xbox/achievements/) showed 4,916 tracked players, 5,713 awards and 15 shared non-introduction awards with positive counts. Award names matched the PS5 list. The launch-refresh model uses the Xbox counts as a weak timing covariate, since platform populations and snapshot times differ.
- The [PS5 individual trophy page](https://www.exophase.com/trophy/grand-theft-auto-vi-psn/1154516-baited-my-tackle) displayed the same aggregate percentage as the game page. The [Wayback availability query](https://archive.org/wayback/available?url=www.exophase.com%2Fgame%2Fgrand-theft-auto-vi-psn%2Ftrophies%2F&timestamp=20261119050000) returned no snapshot for the second practice target.
- Hyndman and Athanasopoulos describe separate evaluation sets, quantile scores and forecast combinations in *Forecasting: Principles and Practice*. The launch-refresh comparison uses fixed development and holdout seeds and checks both individual models and a mixture. [Evaluation](https://otexts.com/fpp3/tscv.html) · [Combinations](https://otexts.com/fpp3/combinations.html)
- Chen and Liu's work on conditional Bernoulli distributions and Biscarri et al.'s work on Poisson-binomial convolution motivated the use of count probabilities conditioned on observed totals. The implementation uses a two-dimensional convolution to track early awards and later integer rounding offsets together. [Poisson-binomial references](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poisson_binomial_distribution#Computational_methods) · [Convolution reference](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.csda.2018.01.007)
